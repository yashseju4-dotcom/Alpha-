Lifetime Support

Our Build Process

  1. Consultation & requirement gathering
  2. Component selection
    • CPU & Motherboard matching
    • GPU & Power supply calculation
    • Cooling solution planning
  3. Assembly with proper cable management
  4. 24–48 hour stress testing
  5. Quality check & packaging

Key Terms

AIO
All-In-One liquid cooler for efficient CPU cooling.
NVMe
High-speed solid-state storage interface (much faster than SATA).
RGB
Customizable lighting system used for aesthetics.
PSU
Power Supply Unit – converts AC to DC power for components.
